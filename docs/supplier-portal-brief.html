Supplier Self-Service Portal

A simple status brief
Owner: Syed Faez Hasan (Seller Experience)
Date: 8 June 2026
In one line: a single place where sellers can see and update their own profile and bank account details — instantly, 24/7 — instead of raising a support ticket.

1. What are we building?

A self-service tool inside the Fleek seller app, with two simple tabs:

  • Profile — shop name, bio, profile picture, contact details, zone & account status.
  • Account & Payments — current bank account, full history, and bank update requests.

Sellers can update what they own. Seller Support reviews bank changes. Approved bank details flow into Fleek's Financial Portal.

2. Why does it matter?

  • Sellers wait 5 minutes to 24+ hours for the same task today, depending on when they ask.
  • There is no visible history — sellers and support both ask "which bank account is active right now?"
  • Bank / KYC is the #1 support theme — 297 → 334 → 281 tickets in Mar / Apr / May '26 — almost all of which are self-serviceable from data we already hold.
  • Old process: Google Form + email chain for bank updates, with no status visibility for the seller.

3. What is already done?

Profile tab (live)

  • Real shop name, bio, profile picture, email, phone, zone and status pulled live from Fleek's database.
  • Inline edit for shop name, bio and profile picture.
  • AI moderation on profile pictures — blocks images containing phone numbers, emails, or external store links (disintermediation defense).
  • Mobile / Desktop view toggle.

Account & Payments tab (live)

  • Current bank account card with default badge.
  • Full history of every bank account the seller has ever used.
  • View details on each row — address, account type, currency, bank code.
  • Active / Inactive labels, added & deactivated dates, total active duration.
  • Country-aware bank update form with field validation and document upload.
  • Status badges: Submitted → Under Review → Approved / Rejected.

Internal admin tool (live)

  • Seller Support queue, one-click Approve / Reject with reason, signed-URL access to documents, full audit log.

5. What we need from Engineering

  1. Platform API access — endpoint URL + service auth token so the portal can push edits back.
  2. Vendor update contract — confirmed mutation for shop name, bio & picture changes.
  3. Bank account push — mutation that lands approved submissions in the Fleek Financial Portal automatically.
  4. OTP service for verifying email & phone changes.
  5. PII retention policy — what we may safely store of account numbers and supporting documents.

Our side is already scaffolded — once items 1–3 are provided, full wiring is under a day of work.

6. How it works

Profile update

Seller edits shop name, bio or profile picture
↓
Validation + AI moderation on pictures (blocks phone, email, external links)
↓
Saved on our side → seller sees confirmation
↓
Pushed to Fleek's vendor record → visible everywhere (app, marketplace, internal tools)

Bank account update

Seller submits new bank account details
↓
Status: Submitted → enters Seller Support queue
↓
Seller Support reviews documents & details (Status: Under Review)
↓
Decision

If rejected

  • Seller is notified of the rejection
  • Existing bank account remains active
  • Seller can correct & resubmit

If approved

  • Seller is notified
  • Details pushed to Fleek Financial Portal
  • New account becomes the default payment account (if applicable)
  • Historical records are retained
  • Seller can view details & history in the tool
