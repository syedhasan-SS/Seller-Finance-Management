#### **FleekOS launches Seller Experience 2.0 — sellers spend less time chasing support and more time growing their business**

*Karachi, 30 September 2026*

FleekOS today announced the completion of **Seller Experience 2.0**, a six-month transformation of how sellers interact with the platform. Sellers can now resolve **8 out of every 10 questions instantly through self-serve**, are notified about payouts, QC holds, and order issues **before they go wrong**, and — for the first time — are paired with a Seller Experience team that helps them **grow**, not just fix tickets.

Until now, FleekOS sellers depended on a small support team for almost every question. With ticket volume rising 20% in a single month and first-response times stretching from under one minute to over half an hour, the model was reaching its limit. More importantly, sellers were spending hours of their week in chat threads instead of running their stores.

Seller Experience 2.0 changes that. It introduces three things sellers can feel immediately: a **Seller Hub** that holds the answer to every common question with one click; a **Seller Concierge** AI that handles routine requests like profile updates, password resets, and bank detail changes without a human in the loop; and **Proactive Alerts** that tell sellers *what's happening* — payout dates, QC outcomes, listing issues — before they have to ask. For Fleek's most engaged sellers, a dedicated **Seller Growth Partner** now reaches out *weekly* with sales insights, demand signals, and category-level guidance.

> *"I used to spend my mornings on the support chat asking when my payout would arrive or why a product was on QC hold. Now FleekOS just tells me — and most of the time, I don't even have to open the app. It feels like Fleek is on my side."*
> — Aisha M., FleekOS seller since 2024

#### How it works

- **Seller Hub** — a redesigned, instrumented help center covering the top 20 question categories (which today drive 80% of Information tickets). Built on real ticket-language, search-optimized, with feedback loops that flag where sellers still get stuck.
- **Seller Concierge AI** — a chat-first AI agent trained on Fleek-specific SOPs that resolves routine requests (profile picture, password, bank details, shop name, BIO updates) end-to-end without escalation.
- **Proactive Alerts** — system-generated notifications that pre-empt the top 5 complaint drivers: payout delays, QC holds, pickup misalignment, refund disputes, and order status changes.
- **Seller Growth Partner** — for the ~600 engaged transacting sellers, a weekly outbound touchpoint with personalized sales, listing, and pricing insights. The Seller Experience team owns this.
- **Continuous Voice-of-Seller** — in-app NPS replaces the periodic Google form. Verbatims are tagged, trended, and reviewed weekly. We measure satisfaction every week, not every quarter.

> *"The old model treated seller support as a cost center. Seller Experience 2.0 treats it as a growth function. We didn't grow the team — we changed what the team builds. Tooba, Hamza, and Basil aren't agents anymore. They're system builders. That's what got us from +1 to +30."*
> — [Sponsor], FleekOS Leadership

#### Availability

Seller Experience 2.0 is now live for all FleekOS sellers across Pakistan and Rest-of-World markets. Sellers can access the Seller Hub at `support.joinfleek.com`, and the Seller Concierge is available inside the FleekOS seller app.
