# Seller Hi-Tea — May 2026

**Date:** Thursday, 8 May 2026
**Format:** 90-min in-person session, Karachi
**Cohort:** 15–20 high-GMV sellers (mix of Detractors + Passives, Q1 2026 NPS wave)
**Hosts:** Faez (SX) + Marketplace Operations lead
**Attendees from Fleek:** Faez, Tooba, Hamza, Basil, MP Ops lead

---

## Why this session

Q1 2026 NPS landed at **+1**. The post-Bakra Eid wave is collected in early June; the report is due **10 June**. Without a visible response to detractor pain *before* that wave, the June score will look structurally identical to Q1. This Hi-Tea is the cheapest, fastest sentiment lever we have in that 5-week window — and the qualitative spine for everything else in the Q2 plan.

It pulls forward the "Detractor Recovery Loop" originally scheduled for Week 11 of [01-q2-execution-plan.md](01-q2-execution-plan.md) and makes it the kickoff act of Q2, co-owned with Marketplace Operations.

## Outcomes we're optimizing for

1. **Listen.** Capture verbatim pain points from 15–20 sellers whose churn or advocacy moves the business.
2. **Commit.** Within 7 days, ship a "You Said / We Did" list of 5–8 commitments with owners + dates.
3. **Lift.** Re-engage every attendee personally before the post-Eid NPS wave hits their inbox.

---

## Selection criteria

From BigQuery, take all sellers who:

1. Are ACTIVE and placed ≥1 order in last 90 days.
2. Responded to Q1 NPS as **Detractor (0–6)** or **Passive (7–8)**.
3. Are PK Zone or PK Non-Zone (Karachi-reachable).
4. Are not in active legal/dispute escalation.
5. Did not attend a prior Hi-Tea in last 6 months.

Rank by **90-day GMV**. Invite the top 40. Target mix: ~60% Detractors / ~40% Passives.
